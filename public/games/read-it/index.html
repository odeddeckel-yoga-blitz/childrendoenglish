Read It!

A free phonics game for children learning to read English, ages 6–10. Each level practises one short vowel (a, i, o, u, e) with three-letter words: first the child reads a word and taps its picture, then hears a word and picks its spelling from three words that differ by one letter. A wrong answer shows the word with the changed letter highlighted and plays it.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com — free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.
