Read It!

A free phonics game for children learning to read English, ages 6–10. The first five levels each practise one short vowel (a, i, o, u, e) with three-letter words, the next three practise magic e and the last two sh, ch, th and ck: first the child reads a word and taps its picture, then hears a word and picks its spelling from three words that differ by one letter. A wrong answer shows the word with the changed letter highlighted and plays it.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com — free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

What are CVC words? Three-letter words made of a consonant, a short vowel and a consonant, like cat, pig, dog, bus and bed. They are usually the first words children learn to sound out.

In what order are the sounds taught? The usual phonics order: first one short vowel per level (a, then i, o, u and e), then magic e (cap → cape, kit → kite, hop → hope), then two letters that make one sound (sh, ch, th, ck). Each level has two parts: read a word and tap its picture, then hear a word and pick its spelling from three words that differ by one letter or one sound.

What age is Read It! for? Children aged about 6–10 who are starting to read English, including children whose first language is Hebrew or Spanish.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in any phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish.
