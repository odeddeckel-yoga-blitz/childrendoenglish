Rhyme Time: a rhyming words game with pictures

A free online rhyming game for kids ages 5 to 9. Each round shows a picture with its word and says it out loud, for example "cat". The child taps the one of three pictures that rhymes with it: hat. A wrong tap plays both words, so the child can hear that the endings are different, and a short tip explains why. Three levels of 8 rounds: short words, longer words, then a mix where the wrong pictures start with the same sound.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com, free English vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

What is a rhyme? Two words rhyme when they end with the same sound, like cat and hat or moon and spoon. The first sound changes and the ending stays the same.

Which rhyming words are in Rhyme Time? Level 1 uses short words (cat, hat, bat; fox, box, socks; pen, hen, ten). Level 2 uses longer words (snake, cake, lake; train, rain; moon, spoon, balloon; bear, pear, chair). Level 3 mixes both, and the wrong pictures start with the same sound or have the same vowel, so the child has to listen to the ending.

Why do some rhymes look different when written? Rhyme is about sound, not spelling. One rhymes with sun, bear rhymes with chair, and juice rhymes with goose. The answers are pictures, so children listen to the words instead of matching letters. Every word is spoken, and a speaker button under each picture says its name.

Is it free, and does it work in Hebrew? Yes. It is free with no ads and no sign-up, and works in any phone, tablet or computer browser. The buttons can be in English, Hebrew or Spanish, so Hebrew-speaking children can practise rhymes in English (חרוזים באנגלית). The words themselves are always English.
