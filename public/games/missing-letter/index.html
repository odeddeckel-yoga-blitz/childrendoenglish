Missing Letter

A free fill-in-the-missing-letter game for kids learning to read and spell in English. Each round shows a picture, says the word and shows it with one letter missing. Your child picks the letter from three or four buttons. Three levels of eight words: short vowels (c_t), first and last letters (_og, be_), and a letter in the middle of a longer word (ze_ra).

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How does the missing letter game work? Each round shows a picture, says the word out loud and shows it with one letter missing, like c_t. Your child taps the letter that fills the gap. After a wrong tap the right letter appears in the word and the word is said again, and your child taps that letter to go on.

What do the three levels practise? Level 1: the short vowel in three-letter words (cat, bed, pig, fox, sun). Level 2: the first or last letter, choosing between letters that look or sound alike (b, d, p; m, n, w; c, k, s). Level 3: a letter in the middle of a longer word (zebra, lemon, robot, donkey).

What age is it for? About 5 to 9, or any child starting to read and spell in English. Level 1 suits kindergarten and first grade. The buttons can be in English, Hebrew or Spanish; the words stay in English.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It runs in any phone, tablet or computer browser. The words are read by a computer voice.
