Sentence Builder

A free sentence building game for kids learning English. Each round shows a picture and shuffled word tiles; the child taps them in order to build the sentence about the picture. Level 1 builds "This is a cat.", level 2 builds "A cat is an animal.", and level 3 adds an extra a or an tile that doesn't belong. Six rounds per level.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How do you play Sentence Builder? Each round shows a picture and some word tiles. The child taps the tiles in order to build the sentence about the picture, such as "This is a cat." A placed tile goes back with a tap. When the sentence is complete, the game checks it and reads it aloud.

What do the three levels practise? Level 1 builds four-word sentences: "This is a cat." Level 2 builds five-word sentences: "A cat is an animal." Level 3 adds one extra tile, a or an, that doesn't belong, so the child has to choose "an apple" but "a fruit".

Can my child hear the sentences? Yes. In levels 1 and 2 the sentence is said at the start of the round, and in every level it is said once it is built. The voice is computer-generated text-to-speech.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in a phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish.
