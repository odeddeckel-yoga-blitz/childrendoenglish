Picture Bingo

A free English vocabulary bingo game for kids ages 5–10. The card has pictures and no words, so children who can't read yet can play. The game calls a word out loud, and the child finds and taps that picture. Get a full row, column or diagonal and it's bingo. Words come from everyday topics: animals, food, vehicles, clothes, things at home, toys and nature.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com, free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How does Picture Bingo work? The game says an English word and the child taps that picture on the bingo card. A wrong tap shakes the picture and the word is said again. When a whole row, column or diagonal is marked, it is bingo and the next card starts.

What are the three cards? Card 1 is 3 by 3 with pictures from two different topics, such as animals and vehicles. Card 2 is 3 by 3 from one topic, so the pictures are closer to each other. Card 3 is 4 by 4 from four topics, and some called words are not on the card: the child taps Not on my card.

Can I use it with a class or print the bingo cards? For a class, turn on Class mode and show the game on a projector or big screen: the card is bigger, the calls are slower and each word is said twice. There are no printable bingo cards; Picture Bingo is an on-screen game.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in any phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish. The words are spoken by a computer voice.
