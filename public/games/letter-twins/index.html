Questions parents ask

Why do children mix up b and d? b, d, p and q are the same shape turned or flipped, and young readers are still learning that direction matters in letters. Mixing them up is common and usually fades with practice.

How does Letter Twins help? Each round shows a picture, plays the English word and asks for its first letter, so the child links the letter shape to its sound. After a mistake it shows the right letter and a short tip for telling the pair apart.
