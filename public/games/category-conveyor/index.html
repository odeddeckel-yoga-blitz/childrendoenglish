Category Conveyor — free English sorting game for kids

Kids ages 6-12 practise English vocabulary by sorting real word photos into category bins: Animals, Food, Transport, Clothing, Home and Colors. Every word is spoken aloud, so children connect the picture, the written word and its sound. Fast, accurate sorting speeds the belt up and unlocks trickier words — like orange, which is a fruit AND a color! Free, no ads, no sign-up. Part of Children Do English, a free vocabulary app for kids with Hebrew support.
