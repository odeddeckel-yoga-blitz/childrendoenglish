Letter Drop

A free arcade spelling game for kids learning English. A picture shows and its word is spoken; letters fall down four lanes and your child catches the ones that spell the word, in order. Three levels of five words, from three-letter words with the letters shown faintly to five-letter words spelled from the picture and the sound. Three hearts per level.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How do you play Letter Drop? A picture shows and its English word is spoken. Letters fall down the screen; your child taps the letters of the word in order, first letter first. Each caught letter fills its space under the word. The speaker button says the word again.

What happens after a wrong letter? A wrong letter, or a letter of the word caught too early, costs one of three hearts, and the letter that comes next appears in its space. Your child still has to catch it. After three wrong letters the same word starts again with three new hearts. Letters that fall off the bottom cost nothing.

What do the three levels practise? Level 1: three-letter words (cat, sun, fox) with the letters shown faintly to match. Level 2: four-letter words (fish, frog, milk) with empty spaces. Level 3: five-letter words (zebra, apple, train). The wrong letters are often look-alikes like b, d and p.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It runs in any phone, tablet or computer browser. The words are read by a computer voice.
