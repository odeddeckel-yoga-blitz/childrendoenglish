Simon Says

A free Simon Says game for kids learning English action verbs. The game says an instruction and the child taps the matching action picture; when the instruction leaves out "Simon says", the right move is to stay still. Level 1 has every instruction with "Simon says", level 2 adds tricky rounds without it, and level 3 gives two actions in a row. Eight rounds per level.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support. Pictures: Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D (MIT licence).

Questions parents ask

How do you play Simon Says? The game says an instruction, such as "Simon says, clap your hands!", and shows it on screen. The child taps the picture of that action. In level 2 some instructions leave out "Simon says": then the right answer is the Stay still button, just like the playground game.

What do the three levels practise? Level 1 is every instruction with "Simon says", three pictures to choose from. Level 2 adds three tricky rounds without "Simon says". Level 3 gives two actions at once, such as "Simon says, run and wave!": tap both, in order.

Which actions are in the game? Sixteen: clap your hands, wave, run, walk, swim, dance, climb, sleep, laugh, cry, yawn, wink, sneeze, hug, write your name and ride a bike. Kids can act them out too.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in a phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish. The voice is computer-generated text-to-speech.
