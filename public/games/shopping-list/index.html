Shopping List

A free listening game for kids learning English food words. The game reads a shopping list out loud and your child picks the foods off the shelf in the same order. Three levels of five lists: two foods from a shelf of six, three from eight, then four from nine. It trains careful listening and short-term memory with the same food words and photos as the rest of the site.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How do you play Shopping List? The game reads a short shopping list out loud, one food at a time, while the numbered spaces in the basket light up. Then your child taps the foods on the shelf in the same order. The list is never written, so it is all listening, and the speaker button reads it again.

What happens after a wrong tap? The picture shakes, the game says the food that comes next and puts a gold ring around it, and your child taps it to go on. Only lists picked right the first time count toward the stars.

What age is it for? About 5 to 10. Level 1 has two foods to remember, level 3 has four. No reading is needed. The buttons can be in English, Hebrew or Spanish; the food words stay in English.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It runs in any phone, tablet or computer browser. The words are read by a computer voice.
