Beginning Sounds

A free beginning sounds game for kids learning English letters. Each round shows a big letter and three pictures; the child listens to the words and taps the one that starts with the letter. Level 1 uses common letters, level 2 all eighteen, and level 3 adds sound-alike traps such as b and p. Eight rounds per level.

Questions parents ask

How do you play Beginning Sounds? Each round shows a big letter and three pictures. The child taps the picture whose word starts with that letter, for example B for bus. Every picture has a speaker button that says its word, and the words appear after the tap with the first letter marked.

What do the three levels practise? Level 1 uses eleven common letters, and the wrong pictures start with sounds that are easy to tell apart. Level 2 uses all eighteen letters. Level 3 adds a sound-alike trap in every round: b or p, d or t, f or v, m or n, g or k, s or z.

Why are some letters missing? The game only uses letters whose usual sound is the first sound of the word, so the letter and the sound always match. Vowels, and starts like ch, sh, th or a soft c (as in circle), are left for later.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in a phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish.
