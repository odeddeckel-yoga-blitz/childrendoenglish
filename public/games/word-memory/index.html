Word Memory

A free memory game for English vocabulary. The cards lie face down: half are pictures, half are English words. Turn over two at a time and find each picture with its word. The word is read aloud when its card turns over and again when the pair is found. Three levels (6, 8 and 12 cards) and six topics: animals, food, toys, home, clothing and transport, or a mixed set. Fewer moves earn more stars.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com, free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents and teachers ask

How do I use Word Memory with a class? Open the page on the projector or interactive board, pick a topic and let children take turns calling out two cards. Every time a picture and its English word are found, the word is read aloud, so the whole class hears it. Level 1 has 6 cards and level 3 has 12, which still fit on one screen.

Which grade is it for? It is not tied to one grade's syllabus. The words are common first English nouns (cat, bread, shirt, bus, chair), so it fits any class that is learning those topics, and older children can race for fewer moves.

Does my child need to read English? It helps a little. Word cards show the written word and say it out loud when they turn over, so a child who is just starting to read can match by sound as well as by spelling.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in any phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish.
