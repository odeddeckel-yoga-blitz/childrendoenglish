Feelings

A free feelings and emotions game for kids learning English, with thirteen feeling words: happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, tired, bored, excited, sick, worried, shy, silly and hungry. Level 1 says a word and shows three faces, level 2 shows a face and asks for its word, and level 3 tells a short story and asks how they feel. Eight rounds per level.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support. Faces: Microsoft Fluent Emoji 3D (MIT licence).

Questions parents ask

How do you play Feelings? Level 1 says a feeling word, such as "happy", and the child taps the matching face. Level 2 shows a face and three words: tap the word that fits. Level 3 tells a little story, such as "I got a new puppy!", and asks how the child in the story feels.

Which feelings are in the game? Thirteen: happy, sad, angry, scared, surprised, tired, bored, excited, sick, worried, shy, silly and hungry. Each level plays eight of them.

Can my child hear the words? Yes. The feeling word and the level 3 story are said aloud, and the speaker buttons say them again. The voice is computer-generated text-to-speech.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in a phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish.
