Odd One Out

A free "which one doesn't belong" game for kids learning English. Each round shows four pictures with their English words: three share a group and one doesn't. After the tap, the game names both groups ("3 animals, 1 food") so the child learns the group words too. Level 1 mixes far-apart groups, level 2 closer ones, and level 3 asks for finer sorting such as fruit or vegetables and farm or sea animals. Eight rounds per level.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How do you play Odd One Out? Each round shows four pictures with their English words. Three belong to the same group and one does not. The child taps the one that doesn't belong, and the game then names both groups, for example "3 animals, 1 food".

What do the three levels practise? Level 1 mixes groups that are far apart: animals, food, vehicles and clothes. Level 2 uses closer groups: food or home things, toys or sports, clothes or school things. Level 3 asks for finer sorting: farm or sea animals, fruit or vegetables, colors or numbers, and vehicles that go on land, on water or in the air.

Can my child hear the words? Yes. Every picture has a speaker button that says its English word, and the odd one is said again after each answer. The voice is computer-generated text-to-speech.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in a phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish.
