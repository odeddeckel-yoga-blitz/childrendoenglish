Blend It

A free blending game for kids learning to read short English words. Each round lights up the letters of a three-letter word one by one (c … a … t), then joins them; your child taps the picture of the word. Three levels of six words: three pictures that look nothing alike, then four pictures with look-alike words (cat, hat), then four pictures with the same vowel and no sound before the tap.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How does Blend It work? Each round shows a three-letter word as letter tiles. The tiles light up one at a time, left to right, then slide together. Your child blends the letters into a word and taps its picture from three or four photos. After a wrong tap the word is said and the letters light up again, and your child picks again.

Does the game say each letter sound? No. The game says the whole word, never single letter sounds. On levels 1 and 2 the word is spoken once the letters have joined. On level 3 nothing is spoken before the tap, so your child reads the word on their own.

What age is it for? About 5 to 8, or any child who knows the letter sounds and is starting to read short words. The buttons can be in English, Hebrew or Spanish; the words stay in English.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It runs in any phone, tablet or computer browser. The words are read by a computer voice.
