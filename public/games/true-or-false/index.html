True or False

A free true or false reading game for kids learning English. Each round shows a picture and one short sentence, and the child decides if it is true. Level 1 names the picture ("This is a cat."), level 2 says what kind of thing it is ("A cat is an animal."), and level 3 tells look-alike groups apart. Eight rounds per level, half true and half false.

No ads, no sign-up. Part of childrendoenglish.com: free vocabulary games, flashcards and quizzes with Hebrew support.

Questions parents ask

How do you play True or False? Each round shows one picture and one short English sentence about it, such as "This is a cat." The child taps True if the sentence matches the picture and False if it doesn't. After the tap, the game shows and says the true sentence.

What do the three levels practise? Level 1 names the picture: "This is a cat." A false sentence names something from another group. Level 2 says what kind of thing it is: "A cat is an animal." (animal, fruit, vegetable, vehicle or toy). Level 3 is harder: a false sentence names something from the same group, such as a fox called a lion.

Can my child hear the sentences? Yes. Every sentence is said at the start of the round and again from the speaker button, and the true sentence is said after each tap. The voice is computer-generated text-to-speech.

Is it free? Yes. No ads, no sign-up and nothing to install. It works in a phone, tablet or computer browser, with the buttons in English, Hebrew or Spanish.
